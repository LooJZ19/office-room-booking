About us

A workspace built around short bookings

Northpoint Workspaces opened in 2021 with one floor and three rooms. We noticed that most people did not want a desk for a month — they wanted a proper room for two hours, with a screen that worked.

Today we run four room types on Jalan Molek, used by consultancies, tuition providers, remote teams and companies interviewing candidates.

Our mission

Make a professional room as easy to book as a table at a restaurant.

Our vision

Workspace in Johor that is priced by the hour, available the same day, and never requires a contract.

Office facilities

Shared amenities available to everyone with a booking:

  • Fibre Wi-Fi, 500 Mbps
  • Pantry and free coffee
  • Reception and guest check-in
  • Printing and scanning
  • Covered parking
  • Accessible entrance and lift
  • Air conditioning throughout
  • Cleaned between bookings

Why choose us

No membership, no deposit, no minimum booking beyond one hour. If a room is free on the timeline, you can have it.
